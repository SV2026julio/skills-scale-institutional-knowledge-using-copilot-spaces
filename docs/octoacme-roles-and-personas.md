# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies. They ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and acceptance criteria validation strategies
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify and triage defects, track resolution
- Validate release readiness against quality gates
- Collaborate with developers on test automation and testability

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable releases
- Reduce post-release defects and rework
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status in delivery syncs
- Test plan reviews with Product and Project leads
- Defect reports and blockers escalation

### Interactions
- Works with **Developers** on test automation and testability improvements
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on defining and validating acceptance criteria
- Partners with **Project Managers** on release readiness and quality gate approvals
- Coordinates with **Security Engineers** on security test validation
- Supports **Release Managers** in confirming quality gates before deployment

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, funding approval, and oversight. They represent business needs and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business requirements and context
- Approve project initiation and budgets
- Review and approve major milestones
- Escalate priority conflicts and remove blockers at sponsor level
- Receive status updates and provide steering input

### Goals
- Ensure project aligns with business strategy
- Maintain organizational alignment and buy-in
- Remove executive-level blockers

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone approval gates
- Escalation and decision requests

### Interactions
- Engages with **Project Managers** for status updates and escalation
- Works with **Product Managers** on prioritization and business alignment
- Receives release communication from **Release Managers**
- Provides governance and approvals across all teams

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define technical strategy, design system components, and guide implementation decisions. They balance innovation with maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review and approve technical designs and major PRs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide team on technical best practices and tooling
- Mentor developers and facilitate knowledge sharing

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team capability and knowledge

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and feedback
- Technical risk escalation

### Interactions
- Collaborates with **Developers** on design, code review, and mentoring
- Escalates technical risks to **Project Managers**
- Partners with **Security Engineers** on architectural security decisions
- Supports **QA/Testing Leads** on testability and automation strategy

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers ensure that OctoAcme projects meet security standards and compliance requirements. They identify risks and drive secure-by-design practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Coordinate security scanning and penetration testing
- Manage security incident response and escalation
- Provide security guidance and training to teams

### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant software
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and breaches
- Build security awareness across teams

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews in planning and pre-release gates
- Security incident reports and escalations
- Security best practice guidance

### Interactions
- Works with **Developers** on secure coding practices
- Collaborates with **Technical Leads / Architects** on architecture security
- Coordinates with **QA/Testing Leads** on security test validation
- Escalates security risks to **Project Managers**

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate and execute release activities, ensuring smooth deployments with minimal risk. They maintain release schedules and documentation.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning and scheduling
- Manage release notes and deployment checklists
- Orchestrate deployment activities across environments
- Monitor deployments and coordinate rollback if needed
- Post-release communication and verification

### Goals
- Execute on-time, low-risk releases
- Maintain clear release communication
- Minimize production incidents and rework

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings
- Pre-release readiness reviews
- Deployment day coordination
- Post-release stakeholder updates

### Interactions
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on timeline and release scheduling
- Works with **QA/Testing Leads** to confirm quality gates are met
- Partners with **Developers** to ensure deployment readiness
- Communicates release status and outcomes to **Stakeholders / Sponsors**
